## Answer first

A new CBA insurance regulation is a **secondary (sub-legislative) normative legal act** issued **not** as a free-standing document but as an **annex (Հավելված) approved by a decision of the CBA Board — «ՀՀ Կենտրոնական բանկի խորհրդի որոշում»** — under authority delegated by the **RA Law "On Insurance and Insurance Activities" (ՀՀ օրենք «Ապահովագրության և ապահովագրական գործունեության մասին», HO-177, 2007)** and the **RA Law "On the Central Bank of the RA"**, and it must obey the drafting/format rules of the **RA Law "On Normative Legal Acts" («Նորմատիվ իրավական ակտերի մասին», HO-180-N, 2018)** (WRS-036, WRS-037, WRS-039, WRS-040). The instrument type is a **«Կանոնակարգ» (Regulation)**; CBA numbers the insurance-sector regulations in a **3/xx series** (e.g. **3/01** licensing/qualification, **3/02** prudential economic norms, **3/03** technical reserves, **3/04** insurer reporting forms/procedure/deadlines) (WRS-043–WRS-048, WRS-049). The standard anatomy is: a **decision preamble citing the empowering law article**, then the Regulation itself organised into numbered **chapters «Գլուխ»** — conventionally opening with **«Գլուխ 1. Ընդհանուր դրույթներ» (General Provisions)** and a concepts/definitions chapter **(հիմնական հասկացություններ / սահմանումներ)** — with continuously numbered **points «կետ»** (sub-divided into parts «մաս» / sub-points «ենթակետ»), reporting forms carried as **annexes «Հավելված»**, and an **entry-into-force clause** (CBA decisions typically enter into force on a stated day after official publication) (WRS-040, WRS-044, WRS-045, WRS-052). The existing insurer reporting rulebook is **Regulation 3/04**, which sets report **forms, the information they contain, and the procedure and deadlines** for electronic submission to the CBA, and has been amended to add ORSA and fraud-related reporting forms (WRS-043, WRS-045, WRS-052). **No insurance-sector-specific CBA incident-reporting «Կանոնակարգ» (dedicated template/deadline for insurers) was located in official sources**; the only Armenia-specific incident duty found is the **horizontal Law "On Cybersecurity" (in force 4 Jan 2026, 72-hour reporting, CBA enforcement for financial firms)** (WRS-034). A drafter should therefore model a new incident-reporting regulation on the **3/04 form-and-deadline pattern** and mark any specific deadline/threshold as a placeholder until confirmed against the primary text.

## What the sources say

### Sub-question 1 — Legal basis & hierarchy; the empowering law and the instrument types

- Insurance in the RA is regulated by a hierarchy: the **Constitution, international treaties, the Civil Code, the Law "On Insurance and Insurance Activities", other laws, and the normative legal acts of the Central Bank adopted on their basis** (WRS-036, WRS-053, WRS-054). This places CBA «Կանոնակարգ» instruments **below** the law and **implementing** it.
- The Law "On Insurance and Insurance Activities" (**ՀՀ օրենք «Ապահովագրության և ապահովագրական գործունեության մասին»**, HO-177, adopted 9 April 2007) makes the CBA the **authorized body (լիազոր մարմին)** for the sector: it is empowered to **receive reports of established forms** from insurers about their activity and financial condition, to **conduct inspections** to verify compliance and the authenticity of submitted reports, and to **develop normative and methodological documents** for insurance activity within the authority the law grants it (WRS-036, WRS-037, WRS-038). *(These are the empowerment mechanisms; the exact article number that carries the reporting-forms delegation was not confirmed from the primary text — see Gaps.)*
- CBA's own rule-making power rests on the **RA Law "On the Central Bank of the RA"**, under which the CBA Board issues normative legal acts (WRS-039).
- The operative instrument is a **«Կանոնակարգ» (Regulation)** **approved by a «ՀՀ Կենտրոնական բանկի խորհրդի որոշում» (CBA Board decision)**. The decision is the enacting act; the Regulation is its **annex**. Decisions carry a number with the **«-Ն» suffix marking a normative act** (e.g. decision «թիվ 311-Ն») (WRS-043, WRS-048).
- The insurance regulations are numbered in a **3/xx series** dedicated to the sector, confirmed across official arlis.am titles and the CBA insurance legal-acts index (WRS-049):
  - **3/01** — «Ապահովագրության ոլորտում լիցենզավորման, գրանցման, համաձայնության, թույլտվության տրամադրման, մասնագիտական համապատասխանության ստուգման և որակավորման կարգը» (licensing, registration, consent, authorization, professional-fitness checks and qualification) (WRS-047).
  - **3/02** — «Ապահովագրական գործունեության հիմնական տնտեսական նորմատիվների չափը, ձևավորման և հաշվարկման կարգը …» (prudential/basic economic norms; CBA Board decision **No. 311-Ն, 2 Oct 2007**) (WRS-048).
  - **3/03** — «Տեխնիկական պահուստների տեսակները, դրանց ձևավորման կարգը» (types of technical reserves and their formation) (WRS-046).
  - **3/04** — «Ապահովագրական ընկերությունների հաշվետվությունների ձևերը, դրանցում ներառվող տեղեկատվությունը, ներկայացնելու կարգը և ժամկետները» (insurer reporting forms, content, procedure and deadlines) (WRS-043, WRS-044, WRS-045).
- Parallel **8/xx** regulations apply cross-sectorally to financial organisations (which include insurers): **8/03** information disclosure (WRS-050) and **8/05** business-conduct rules (WRS-051).

### Sub-question 2 — Standard STRUCTURE / anatomy of a CBA «Կանոնակարգ» (Armenian headings)

- The format is governed by the **RA Law "On Normative Legal Acts"** («Նորմատիվ իրավական ակտերի մասին», HO-180-N, adopted 21 March 2018), which prescribes the structural units — **preamble (նախաբան), chapters (գլուխ), articles (հոդված) for laws, points (կետ) for sub-legislative acts, parts (մաս), sub-points (ենթակետ)**, and **appendices/annexes (հավելված)** — plus entry-into-force and legislative-technique rules (WRS-040, WRS-041). The predecessor **Law "On Legal Acts" (2002)** established the same drafting-technique tradition (WRS-042).
- In practice a CBA insurance «Կանոնակարգ» is assembled as:
  1. **The Board decision (որոշում)** — a preamble that **cites the empowering law and article** ("Հիմք ընդունելով … օրենքի … հոդվածը …" / "ղեկավարվելով …"), an operative clause **approving («հաստատել») the Regulation as an annex**, and a **closing entry-into-force clause** (WRS-043, WRS-044).
  2. **The Regulation (Կանոնակարգ)** organised into numbered **chapters «Գլուխ»**. The corroborated convention is to open with **«Գլուխ 1. Ընդհանուր դրույթներ» (General Provisions)** — stating scope and subject — followed by a **concepts/definitions chapter (e.g. «Գլուխ 2», main concepts «հիմնական հասկացություններ» / definitions «սահմանումներ»)** where each defined term is introduced ("Սույն կանոնակարգում օգտագործվող հասկացություններն ունեն հետևյալ նշանակությունը …") (WRS-044, and the standard "Chapter 1 General Provisions / Chapter 2 Concepts and Definitions" pattern seen across CBA regulations, WRS-050, WRS-051).
  3. **Substantive chapters** in continuously numbered **points «կետ»** (a point may split into parts «մաս» and sub-points «ենթակետ»), using self-reference phrasing such as **«սույն կանոնակարգով»** ("by this Regulation") and **«սույն կանոնակարգի … կետ»** (WRS-045).
  4. **Transitional / final provisions and the entry-into-force clause.** CBA Board decisions conventionally state the act **enters into force on a specified day following official publication** (a commonly used formula is "**ուժի մեջ է մտնում պաշտոնական հրապարակման օրվան հաջորդող տասներորդ օրը**" — the tenth day after official publication), unless the decision sets another date (WRS-040, WRS-044).
  5. **Annexes «Հավելված»** — the actual **report forms/tables** are attached as numbered annexes to the Regulation rather than embedded in the point text (WRS-043, WRS-045).

### Sub-question 3 — Existing CBA regulations on REPORTS submitted by insurers

- **Regulation 3/04** is the insurer reporting rulebook: it establishes the **forms of the reports (հաշվետվությունների ձևերը)** that insurance companies operating in the RA submit **to the CBA**, the **information included in them (դրանցում ներառվող տեղեկատվությունը)**, and the **procedure and deadlines for submission (ներկայացնելու կարգը և ժամկետները)** (WRS-043, WRS-044, WRS-045). It is a live instrument maintained through amending Board decisions (WRS-044).
- Content added by amendment (per the CBA's 2014 supervision report): **new reporting forms** for insurers to report **the calculations of responsible/appointed actuaries, professional opinions and assessments, the results of Own Risk and Solvency Assessment (ORSA), and fraud committed in the insurance sector** (WRS-052). This shows the CBA's pattern of **adding a new reporting obligation by inserting a form/point into 3/04** — directly relevant to grounding an incident-reporting form.
- The reporting/publication framework is complemented by **Regulation 8/03** (information disclosure by financial organisations and financial groups) (WRS-050).
- **Not confirmed:** the exact **submission deadlines and frequency** (daily / monthly / quarterly / annual) and the specific **electronic-submission channel** named in 3/04 were not readable from the primary text via the available access method — see Gaps.

### Sub-question 4 — Operational-risk / ICT / information-security / incident requirements, and the Cybersecurity Law interaction

- The CBA has, per its own supervision reporting, established **information-security requirements for financial organisations** (including insurers) with **minimum requirements for managing IT-field risks**, developed with reference to the **ISO/IEC 27001** information-security standard, with a certification/compliance expectation (WRS-052). *(This is described in a CBA report; the specific standalone regulation number for insurers was not located — see Gaps.)*
- **No insurance-sector-specific CBA incident-reporting «Կանոնակարգ»** (a dedicated insurer incident template, deadline or severity threshold) was found in official sources. This is consistent with the finding in **WRB-003**.
- The only Armenia-specific incident-reporting duty located is the **horizontal Law "On Cybersecurity"**, in force **4 January 2026**, which imposes **72-hour reporting** of a serious cyber incident to an **Authorized Body**, with the **CBA enforcing operational-resilience obligations on financial organisations** via supervision (WRS-034). A new CBA insurance incident-reporting regulation would sit **as sector-specific secondary regulation alongside/beneath that horizontal law**, and should cross-reference it rather than duplicate its definitions.

### Sub-question 5 — Drafting / style conventions

- **Instrument & enactment:** draft as a **«Կանոնակարգ»** approved by, and annexed to, a **CBA Board decision**; the decision preamble names the empowering law/article; the number takes the sector prefix (insurance = **3/xx**) and the decision carries the **«-Ն»** normative suffix (WRS-043, WRS-048, WRS-049).
- **Numbering:** hierarchical — **Գլուխ (chapter) → կետ (point, numbered continuously through the regulation) → մաս (part) → ենթակետ (sub-point)** (WRS-040, WRS-045).
- **Defined terms:** collected in a dedicated concepts chapter ("Սույն կանոնակարգում օգտագործվող հասկացություններն ունեն հետևյալ նշանակությունը …"), then used consistently; cross-references use **«սույն կանոնակարգի … կետ/գլուխ»** (WRS-044, WRS-045).
- **Imperative / obligation phrasing:** obligations are expressed with mandatory verbs such as **«պարտավոր է» (is obliged to)**, **«ներկայացնում է» / «ներկայացվում է» (submits / is submitted)**, **«սահմանվում է» (is established)** — declarative-imperative present tense typical of Armenian normative drafting (WRS-045, WRS-052).
- **Deadlines:** expressed as periods in **«ժամկետ»** terms (e.g. "…ոչ ուշ, քան …" — "no later than …"; day/working-day counts), and set in the reporting «Կանոնակարգ» itself (the 3/04 pattern) (WRS-043, WRS-045).
- **Forms & annexes:** report templates are attached as **«Հավելված»** (numbered annexes) to the regulation, referenced from the relevant point (WRS-043, WRS-045).
- **Entry into force:** a closing clause tied to **official publication** (commonly the tenth day after publication) or a specified date (WRS-040, WRS-044).
