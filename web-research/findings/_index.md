# Research Brief Registry (WRB-NNN)

Every research brief produced from web sources is registered here, with a stable
`WRB-NNN` id. The briefs themselves are files in this folder
(`findings/WRB-NNN-*.md`); this table is the index over them. Read before creating
(next free `WRB` id); update after the brief is written.

These ids are **local to `web-research/`** — distinct from the global `FND-NNN` in
`analysis/`. IDs are never reused or deleted — mark a superseded brief `Deprecated`
with a reason and point to the id that replaced it.
